The business case

Train your team, or hire AI engineers from outside?

External AI hires are scarce, expensive and start from zero on your domain. Your own people already understand your operations, your data and your regulations. Upskilling them is the faster, lower-cost and longer-lasting route.

Option A

Hire external AI engineers

  • ✕
    Long, costly recruitmentSpecialist AI talent is scarce in the region and commands premium packages.
  • ✕
    No domain knowledgeMonths spent learning your processes, systems and terminology before delivering value.
  • ✕
    Capability sits with a few peopleA small team becomes a bottleneck for every department's request.
  • ✕
    Knowledge walks out the doorWhen a hire leaves, the know-how and the solutions often leave too.
Option B · Recommended

Upskill your own team with DDS

  • ✓
    A fraction of the costOne programme upskills a whole team for less than the cost of a single specialist hire.
  • ✓
    Domain experts who now use AIYour people apply AI to problems they already understand deeply, from day one.
  • ✓
    Capability spreads across departmentsHR, finance, operations and IT each gain people who can lead AI work.
  • ✓
    Solutions you ownTeams leave with projects, code and know-how that stay inside your organisation.
